## 📦 What's Inside

| Skill | What It Does | Who It's For |
|:---|:---|:---|
| 📞 **Sales Call Research** | One-page pre-call battle map: kill shot, conversation strategy, objection playbook, risk radar | B2B Sales Reps, SDRs, AEs |
| 📊 **Agency Client Report** | Screenshot-ready weekly reports: the story behind the numbers, client language, forward-looking moves | Marketing agencies, freelancers |
| 🛡️ **Competitor Battle Card** | Win strategy for competitive deals: acknowledge-then-redirect, subtext reading, landmine map | Sales teams facing competitor objections |

---

## 🎯 Philosophy

Every section answers one question: **"What do I DO with this information?"**

- **Not** "Here's your data" → **"Here's what happened, why, and what we're doing about it"**
- **Not** "They have these features" → **"They're good at X — but for what YOU need, here's why we're different"**
- **Not** "Company overview" → **"Here's your kill shot, conversation flow, and how to handle their objections"**
