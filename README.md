# 📞 Sales Call Research Assistant

> Turn 20 minutes of pre-call Googling into a 2-minute automated briefing.

An AI skill that researches companies, finds recent news, identifies decision makers, and generates personalized opening hooks — everything a sales professional needs before a call.

## 🎯 What It Does

1. Researches company background, products, funding, and size
2. Finds recent news to create natural conversation hooks
3. Identifies decision makers and their backgrounds
4. Hypothesizes likely pain points based on industry
5. Generates a personalized opening hook for the call

## 📦 Install

Copy `SKILL.md` to your Claude/Codex/ChatGPT skills directory.

Or find it on [skillsmp.com](https://skillsmp.com)

## ⚡ Quick Start

Just say:
> "Prepare a briefing for Stripe before my sales call"

The skill handles the rest.

- B2B Sales Representatives
- SDRs / BDRs
- Account Executives
- Freelance sales consultants
