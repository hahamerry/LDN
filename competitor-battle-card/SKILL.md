# Competitor Battle Card Generator

Generates a one-page battle card that sales teams can use
to handle competitor objections in real time. Designed for
B2B sales reps, SDRs, and account executives who need quick
ammo when a prospect mentions a competitor.

## When to Use

- A prospect says "We're also evaluating [Competitor X]"
- You're preparing for a competitive deal
- You're building a competitive enablement library for your team
- You need objection handling scripts for a specific competitor

## Execution Workflow

### Phase 1: Gather Intel
1. Ask: "Which competitor? And what's your product?"
2. Research the competitor with web search:
   - "competitor name" pricing features reviews G2
   - "competitor name" vs alternatives pros cons
   - "competitor name" weaknesses complaints reddit
   - "competitor name" target market ideal customer

### Phase 2: Analyze & Structure
Extract and organize into the battle card:

1. **Competitor Snapshot** (3 lines)
   - Founding year, funding, target market, key products

2. **Strengths** (what they do well — be honest)
   - 2-3 genuine strengths so reps don't get blindsided

3. **Weaknesses** (our ammunition)
   - 3-5 exploitable gaps: pricing, missing features, complexity,
     support quality, scalability limits, hidden costs

4. **Pricing Traps** (where they overcharge)
   - Hidden fees, per-user vs flat-rate, usage-based surprises

5. **Objection Handling Scripts** (the gold)
   - 3-4 scripted responses for common prospect statements
   - Each includes: what prospect says → rep's response

### Phase 3: Format & Deliver

# Battle Card: [Competitor Name] vs [Our Product]

## 🎯 Competitor at a Glance
[2-3 lines — what they are, who they serve]

## 💪 Their Strengths
1. [Strength] — *Counter: [how to neutralize]*
2. [Strength] — *Counter: [how to neutralize]*

## 🔍 Where They Fall Short
1. [Weakness + evidence]
2. [Weakness + evidence]
3. [Weakness + evidence]

## 💰 Pricing Landmines
- [Hidden cost 1]
- [Hidden cost 2]

## 🗣️ Objection Playbook

| Prospect Says | You Say |
|:---|:---|
| "They're cheaper" | [Response with ROI argument] |
| "We already use them" | [Response about switching cost vs opportunity] |
| "They have feature X" | [Response about feature quality or workaround] |
| "Our team knows them" | [Response about learning curve, support] |

## ⚡ Winning Move
[1-2 sentence killer strategy — what to emphasize to win the deal]

## Execution Rules

- **Be fair to competitors**: Exaggerating weaknesses hurts credibility.
  Prospects will fact-check.
- **Cite sources**: Every claim needs a URL (G2 reviews, Reddit threads, etc.)
- **Quantify when possible**: "$50/user/mo vs our $199 flat" beats "they're expensive"
- **Tone**: Professional, not snarky. This is for internal sales use.
- **Time**: Generate in under 3 minutes.

## Quality Standards

| Criterion | Good | Bad |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Accuracy | Claims backed by reviews/pricing pages | "They suck at everything" |
| Actionability | Rep can read and use immediately | Vague generalizations |
| Specificity | Named features, exact prices | "They have less features" |
| Professionalism | Fair to competitor, confident in us | Dismissive or arrogant |

## Edge Cases

- **Little-known competitor**: Flag "limited public information" and recommend
  discovery call recording + Win/Loss analysis instead
- **Multiple competitors in one deal**: Generate separate cards, don't mix
- **Product changes frequently**: Note "Last updated: [date]" prominently
- **Our product not specified**: Ask user to define their product first
