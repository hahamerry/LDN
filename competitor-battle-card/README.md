# ⚔️ Competitor Battle Card Generator

> Stop losing deals to competitors you don't understand.

An AI skill that generates one-page competitive battle cards for sales teams. Research competitors, find their weak spots, and arm your reps with objection-handling scripts — all in under 3 minutes.

## 🎯 What It Does

1. Researches competitor pricing, features, and reviews
2. Identifies honest strengths (so reps don't get blindsided)
3. Finds exploitable weaknesses backed by evidence
4. Highlights pricing landmines and hidden costs
5. Generates a complete objection-handling playbook

## 📦 Install

Copy `SKILL.md` to your Claude/Codex/ChatGPT skills directory.

## ⚡ Quick Start

Just say:
> "Generate a battle card for Salesforce vs our CRM"

The skill handles the rest.

## 🛠️ Built For

- B2B Sales Reps
- SDRs / BDRs
- Sales Enablement Teams
- Competitive Intelligence Analysts
